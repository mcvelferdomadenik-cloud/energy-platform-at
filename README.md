# MegaVolt

An Austrian electricity supplier that does not exist, run as carefully as one that does.

Our customers belong to an energy community that shares its own solar power. Every 15 minutes the
community takes what it needs, and we sell the rest. So we do not sell consumption, we sell
whatever is *left of it*. Sunny afternoon? Our volume vanishes, and nobody used less.

We still had to buy that volume yesterday. Every quarter hour we guessed wrong, the grid operator
sends us a bill.

## Main purpose of the project

When a customer asks for a price, the sales team usually has to wait for an analyst to pull the
numbers together. This project builds the program that removes that wait: sales sits with the
customer, compares a fixed price with a dynamic one, and sees the offer and the reasons behind it
right away. Every number comes from the program, not from someone's spreadsheet.

## So far

- Real Austrian electricity prices, collected every day
- An energy community of 600 members, 350 of them our customers
- Their meter readings, arriving as messily as real ones do: late, corrected, incomplete
- One value we can trust for every customer and every quarter hour

Next: what each customer really costs us, and then the pricing program itself, which will be hidden because we don't want our competitors knowing too much :)
