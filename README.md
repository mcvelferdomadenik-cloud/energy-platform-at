# MegaVolt

An Austrian electricity supplier that does not exist, run as carefully as one that does.

Our customers belong to an energy community that shares its own power. Every 15 minutes the
community takes what it needs — and we sell the leftovers. So we do not sell consumption, we sell
whatever is *left of it*. Sunny afternoon? Our volume vanishes, and nobody used less.

We still had to buy that volume yesterday. Every quarter hour we guessed wrong, the grid operator
sends us a bill.

So the project works out two things:

- what each customer actually costs us, quarter hour by quarter hour
- whether they are better off on our fixed price, or the dynamic one that tracks the market

Real Austrian prices from [ENTSO-E](https://transparency.entsoe.eu/). Invented customers — a
15-minute profile shows when you are home, and nobody publishes those.

**Phase 0:** prices come in. The rest is on its way.
